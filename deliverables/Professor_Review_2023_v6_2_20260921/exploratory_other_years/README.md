# Separate other-year exploration — excluded from main pilot

Seven Phoenix passes from 2019, 2024 and 2025 were processed during a broader sampling investigation. The main pilot was subsequently clarified as 2023 only. All seven have zero weight in the main pilot. Their original data and results are retained locally, and point estimates remain sealed.

The accompanying table provides identities and precision only. The native-cell support table refers to the earlier multiyear design, including its 2023 comparison passes; it is not the current main-pilot sample. More years can add matched month/time opportunities, but introduce interannual and context-vintage differences and do not fill missing 2023 combinations.

Any adoption of a multiyear main analysis requires a separate, explicit study-design decision. These results are not evidence that the current 2023 pilot has eighteen passes or twelve balanced endpoints.
