# Item 2 — Pass-equivalent formula and interpretation

For retained physical pass *i*:

`w_i = n_clear_pixels_independent_of_view_i / n_domain_pixels_cloud_i`

The reported pass-equivalent exposure is:

`PE = sum_i(w_i)`

Direct recomputation from all 219 retained physical passes gives
`PE = 159.087084046233`. For the 102 individual passes in the 10–12 or 16–18
local-solar-time strata before empirical matching, it gives
`PE = 72.6890289952244`.

This is a secondary clear-domain exposure diagnostic, not an independent
sample size or a count of atmospheric situations. Eight cloudy passes remain
eight physical passes. Cloud cover belongs in first-stage measurement
precision. Usable tree/reference pixel counts are not yet available and are
not represented as zero; they remain assigned to the pre-outcome G3A yield
checkpoint after a sampling design is frozen.

Source: `docs/v2/hitl/G1_OBSERVATION_RECONCILIATION/pass_quality_219.csv`.
