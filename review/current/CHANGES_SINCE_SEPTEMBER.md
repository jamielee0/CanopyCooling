The key change in interpretation is the sensitivity of magnitude. The Phoenix fixed-date time contrast retains its negative direction in the original, common and quadratic checks, but changes from −0.325 to −0.106 or −0.433 K/+10pp. The common footprint retains only 35–57% of original cells and changes context. The result supports a bounded observational pilot finding. It does not resolve a general summer time effect, city ranking or causal mechanism.
