## Main findings to discuss

- All sixteen sampled passes show positive canopy-associated mixed-pixel surface cooling, with individual existing 8 km spatial intervals above zero.
- On the five selected Phoenix dates, the afternoon-minus-morning contrast is −0.325 K/+10pp under the original linear analysis, −0.106 on the common footprint and −0.433 with the original-population quadratic model. Their conditional spatial intervals exclude zero, while their magnitudes differ substantially.
- Footprint and imposed registration shifts change the estimates. Temperature and fixed-reference energy equivalent also differ numerically. Temperature remains primary.
- Sparse independent dates and imperfect synthetic coverage prevent a general summer or causal time claim. Tasks 5 and 6 remain open beyond the prepared proposal and draft materials.
